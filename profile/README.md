### Overview
An innovative fintech platform designed to help Brazilian middle-class investors safely enter the Stocks Market through intelligent, algorithimically and AI driven portfolio management. Our solution addresses the common barriers that prevent everyday Brazilians from investing: lack of time to study economics, fear of investment scams, the supossed complexity in the Stocks Market, which puts them in traditional investment methods like INSS, Renda Fixa, and Poupança, which have little to no return over time. Our intention is to help users invest based on proven value-based strategies to pick stocks, manage Dollar-Cost Average (DCA) and maximize Buy & Hold, decreasing the user's FOMO and fear of the market through automations like those mentioned below, educating the user in the proccess about the Stocks Market.

The platform features a series of custom models and algorithms called Musa (Mansa's Unified Systems for Analysis) trained on robust datasets and algorithms related to the Brazilian Stock Market and economics. Our system analyzes market patterns and trends, builds and manages portfolios and gives an in-depth analysis through our models and RAG features, like the Aladdin by BlackRock, Inc.

This project is being developed and is proposed by [@heitorrosa](https://github.com/heitorrosa) and [@gomesdevs](https://github.com/gomesdevs).
